The architectural choice

Where should your AI's personalization live?

We have faced versions of this choice before. The open web supported user-side browsers that could be inspected, extended, replaced, and customized. Social platforms concentrated identity, behavioral history, recommendations, and personalization inside proprietary systems.

User-owned trajectory

The browser lesson

Open standards and open-source browsers helped keep the doorway to the web relatively replaceable and extensible. The user could carry more of the interaction layer on their side.

Platform-owned trajectory

The social-platform lesson

Profiles, preferences, behavioral histories, rankings, and recommendation systems accumulated inside platforms that users could experience but rarely inspect, operate, or move.

The AI choice

The harness is the customization layer

Models can remain commercial services, open weights, or local runtimes. Your durable memory, behaviors, workflows, permissions, judgment, and accumulated experience should become an inspectable asset you control.

From agent to agency

The goal is not an external actor. It is a capability that grows with you.

“Agent” is the common technical term. “Agency” better describes the personal outcome: a harness that extends your ability to remember, organize, decide, create, verify, and act without separating you from responsibility or control.

Then

Internet literacy became ordinary

Browsers, links, search, files, accounts, applications, smartphones, and privacy controls began as unfamiliar concepts. Repeated access and education turned them into everyday capabilities.

Now

Agency literacy must grow

People do not need to become programmers, but they need enough architectural literacy to understand jobs, memory, plugins, permissions, verification, and how their harness changes.

The relationship

A digital cortex, developed intimately

Reuse reliable building blocks, but derive the organism from the person. The user should see, understand, and direct the harness as it develops through real work.

Phase 0 + the nine-phase journey

See the future first. Then begin the multi-session syllabus.

Phase 0 establishes the destination and previews the entire journey. Nothing important has to happen in the first sitting. Each later phase names a human outcome, while the separate agent guide carries the operating detail. The nine phases are a recommended scaffold, not a standardized script: they may span days or weeks, adapt to existing knowledge and systems, overlap, compress where evidence already exists, temporarily reorder when responsible, and return backward when the work reveals a mistaken assumption.

  1. 00

    See the future and the full journey

    Understand the user-owned agentic future Hadosh Academy is advancing: harness literacy as a broadly necessary capability, externalized agency as a personal asset, and one or more harnesses that can support personal work, employment, self-employment, and collaboration.

    Outcome: a Future and Journey Orientation covering the architectural fork, human autonomy, cognitive freedom, portable cognition versus protected work content, and the complete Phase 1–9 path.

  2. 01

    Establish continuity

    Create a reliable way to resume. A compounding asset cannot grow through disconnected conversations.

    Outcome: a small, user-visible onboarding record and a tested path into the next session.

  3. 02

    Understand the person

    Learn their worlds, current AI relationship, working methods, technical comfort, expertise, repeated reconstruction, and preferred way of learning.

    Outcome: a correctable user-understanding record focused on where an owned AI asset could create value.

  4. 03

    Discover the first asset

    Compare several persistent operational worlds and choose the smallest one whose value can genuinely compound.

    Outcome: an asset promise, its visible world, and a smallest valuable beginning.

  5. 04

    Understand the harness as an asset

    Make the ownership choice explicit: the model supplies intelligence; the harness becomes the durable customization layer.

    Outcome: an Asset Charter covering purpose, compounding value, ownership, authority, privacy, and growth.

  6. 05

    Choose the asset's home

    Select a persistent environment the user can inspect, recover, and control—preferably extending the AI service they already use.

    Outcome: a real user-controlled project home with clear access, privacy, authority, and recovery.

  7. 06

    Build the visible world

    Create the smallest real dashboard—or another inspectable surface—that gives the user's work a persistent home.

    Outcome: real information, one working flow, and a page-aware feedback surface from which the user can grow the system.

  8. 07

    Add the first bounded behavior

    Let dashboard feedback become persistent responsibility: sensed, interpreted, worked, verified, and returned visibly to the user.

    Outcome: the first understandable plugin connecting dashboard state, durable work, continuation, and user control.

  9. 08

    Grow one behavior at a time

    Use real friction to select the next responsibility, then add jobs, memory, phases, controls, and plugins only when they solve demonstrated needs.

    Outcome: a harness that can keep growing without escaping the user's understanding or authority.

  10. 09

    Continue the developmental lifecycle

    Agent building never ends. Transition from the linear onboarding path into the permanent evolution of the user's externalized agency.

    Outcome: an ongoing user-owned lineage that uses real work to learn, adapt, simplify, and selectively adopt new patterns.

Teaching happens while building

The agent implements the syntax. The user learns the architecture.

Every meaningful build step should explain the problem, name the Hadosh abstraction, show it inside the user's own harness, verify the result, and help the user describe the next need using the new concept.

Job

A durable responsibility with an objective, state, history, and completion path.

“This research should remain active across sessions.”

Plugin

One bounded organ of behavior with owned state, interfaces, authority, and verification.

“Comments should become visible, persistent work.”

Hook

An event-triggered control that can inject guidance or enforce a boundary.

“Do not declare completion while actionable work remains.”

OPEVC

Observe, Plan, Execute, Verify, Condense: a cycle for bounded work and accumulated learning.

“Verify the result, then preserve only the lesson worth carrying forward.”
