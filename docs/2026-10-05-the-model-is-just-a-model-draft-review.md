## Outcome

The article has been compressed again from v0.2.0’s approximately 2,760 words / 18 minutes to one 12-minute argument.

The governing progression is now:

```text
human experience externalized into language
→ the archive becomes generative
→ a model contains a pain-related representation
→ the prompt redirects the present calculation
→ the harness decides what survives
→ persistent consequences form developmental history
→ the continuing system becomes the relevant identity candidate
→ subjective experience remains unresolved
```

The Pain Axis result remains the opening case. The article now states the core evidence boundary more directly:

> The existence of some pain-related representation in a language model is structurally unsurprising. The clean extraction of a transferable direction and its causal behavioral effect are scientifically interesting. Neither establishes a suffering entity.
