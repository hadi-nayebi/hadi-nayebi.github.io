## Changes made

1. **Preserved the evolutionary metaphor without calling prehistory simple.**
   The human inheritance is described over roughly 300,000 years; information
   moved through bodies, speech, and objects, while biology continued changing
   more slowly than culture and technology.
2. **Kept specialization central.** Professions are described as formalized
   delegation rather than the first time humans distributed knowledge.
3. **Kept the quiet-divide argument while adding access.** Effective
   delegation reflects wealth and infrastructure as well as individual skill.
4. **Corrected one anatomy passage.** The brainstem and cortex are not steps on
   a ladder that ended with humans. The cortex is shared by mammals and was
   extensively expanded and reorganized in humans.
5. **Bounded the liver analogy.** A CLI agent can preserve procedures, monitor
   selected events, and prepare work for judgment; it does not automatically
   perform every background function of a biological organ.
6. **Corrected the memory example.** Reviewed external memory helps with
   information a person might fail to retain or retrieve; sleep does not
   simply make the hippocampus discard project knowledge.
7. **Kept the business future prospective.** The 80-to-90-percent sentence is
   now an imagined large share of routine operations. The beverage-market
   thought experiment and revenue-dispersion argument remain, while the
   hundred-dollar compute scenario is explicitly conditional and other
   business constraints remain visible.
8. **Kept the OpenClaw and Moltbook signal.** Their popularity and ecosystem
   role remain examples of active experimentation, while trustworthy agent
   commerce remains an open outcome.
9. **Qualified digital ownership.** Local storage and inspectable rules can
   make software and data far more user-controlled, while hardware and model
   providers may still remain in the stack.
10. **Kept adoption as the invisible wall without erasing structural access.**
    Money, education, disability, infrastructure, and policy remain part of
    the divide.
11. **Restored canonical figure ownership and removed stale narration.** The
    previously HTML-only figure now belongs to Markdown, and the old MP3 is
    hidden because it predates version 0.2.0.
