## Changes made

1. **Kept AGI as a prospective argument.** The opening identifies the essay's
   practical target as AGI-like and removes only the fixed monthly-cost claim.
2. **Preserved complex-system thinking.** The contrast is now with a fixed
   artifact and outcome, acknowledging that engineered structures can adapt.
3. **Clarified context loss.** Earlier tokens may leave active context through
   compaction, pruning, or a new session; a larger generator still does not
   create durable project memory by itself.
4. **Kept the RNA metaphor with a correct relationship.** RNA is described as
   a family that includes information carriers and catalytic ribozymes. The
   giant-molecule thought experiment and organism analogy remain.
5. **Restored the Thousand Brains passage as an architectural metaphor.**
   Cortical columns learn associations among sensing, movement, and expected
   outcomes; LLMs learn associations in linguistic and semantic space. The
   mechanisms are explicitly different, while the shared pattern of learned
   relationships, specialization, and coordination keeps its force.
6. **Made engine replacement conditional.** Diffusion language models remain
   an example of changing engines; adapters may change while much of the
   file-backed structure persists.
7. **Corrected Ralph Loop's concrete behavior.** It repeats through a Stop hook
   until a completion signal, limit, or cancellation. Repetition sustains work
   but does not guarantee quality.
8. **Bounded the heartbeat guarantee.** Stateful jobs can resist premature
   closure where the runtime supports it, with retries, cancellation, and
   recovery. Required evidence becomes durable before closing; it does not
   make forgetting impossible.
9. **Narrowed hook recording.** Hooks perceive and record the supported events
   they are configured to capture. The sensory-layer metaphor remains.
10. **Kept growth under user authority.** Reviewed patterns, operations, and
    guardrails may accumulate without implying uncontrolled self-modification.
11. **Kept the one-year scenario as a thought experiment.** The professional
    memory emerges only if logging and review preserve useful lessons; no fixed
    note count is promised.
12. **Corrected the lesson from early agents.** AutoGPT and BabyAGI illustrate
    limits of rigid loops without proving that engineering itself cannot
    produce durable autonomy.
13. **Restored canonical figure ownership and removed stale narration.** The
    Seed figure now belongs to Markdown, and the old MP3 remains hidden because
    it predates version 1.2.0.
