Modernize **We Could Have Had AGI By Now** without changing its governing philosophy. Improve reader carry, pacing, mature harness terminology, and its role between Essay 1 and Essay 3.

## Editorial changes

Essay 1 already established model versus harness. Essay 2 now recovers that distinction briefly, then moves directly to:

## Whole-trilogy critical review — issue 2 resolved

The trilogy review found that Essay 2 was carrying too much mechanism-level detail for its role. The architecture thesis remains unchanged; the implementation examples are now subordinate to it.

Applied changes:

- compressed the job section into one example of a durable primitive rather than a job-system tutorial;
- compressed hooks into the runtime-neutral role of lifecycle interception rather than an inventory of events;
- retained the explicit consolidation causal chain while reducing its implementation catalog;
- reframed the practical roadmap as five architectural categories, with mechanism depth deferred to later technical essays;
- preserved the title, biology/RNA-world argument, Circle of Competence, personal-software transition, seed-agent framing, narrower-operating-model possibility, professional comparison, and organism ending;
- reduced the canonical article from 3,649 to 3,431 words (6.0%) and adjusted the displayed reading time from 22 to 21 minutes.

The reader-facing model is now more dominant:

> shared primitives + differentiated responsibilities + feedback + accumulated experience → unique personal harness organism.

This resolves whole-trilogy issue 2 without flattening the voice or removing the mechanisms that make the architecture concrete. Issue 3—claim-mode discipline—remains the next conceptual review unit.
