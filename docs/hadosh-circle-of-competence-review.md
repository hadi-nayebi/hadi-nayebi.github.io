## Lens origin and purpose

The article uses Warren Buffett’s 1996 Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter as the public source tradition. The original claim concerns selected businesses an investor can evaluate and the importance of knowing the circle’s boundary.

The Hadosh adaptation asks which person, model, plugin, harness mechanism, or deterministic program has demonstrated enough competence for a responsibility under current conditions and authority. It does not attribute the four-circle architecture to Buffett or Berkshire.

NIST’s AI Resource Center supplies a compatible testing, evaluation, verification, and validation discipline. It is supporting evidence for inspectable boundaries, not a second origin claim.

## Unique contribution

- **First Principles:** identify the function.
- **The Map Is Not the Territory:** keep its representation corrigible.
- **Circle of Competence:** assign the responsibility to bounded capability and define the exit.

The third lens adds responsibility routing. It does not restate decomposition or corrigibility as its thesis.

Hadi's Issue #164 correction made responsibility attributable at each cognitive step. Hadi's later Issue #186 question exposed the necessary distinction: responsibility is the assignment and accountable owner; competence is the evidence that the assignee can perform under stated conditions. Version 0.11.0 removed the proxy/synonym claim. Version 0.12.0 makes the division of cognitive work explicit before discussing word choice: decompose a job, assign each part by demonstrated competence, record responsibility and checks, diagnose the cause of a failed check, then repair the responsible component or the routing/verification boundary. Nested plugin, hook, and harness boundaries are acknowledged. Failure at a check is a starting location for diagnosis, not proof that the assigned component caused it.

## September 28 conceptual correction

Hadi asked that Circle of Competence be the assignment lens for a cognitive load shared across the person, general and specialized models, harness, plugins, and hooks. The article now states this early and distinguishes an observed failure from its cause. The corrective target can be retrieval, inference, a plugin contract, harness routing, verification, or an unresolved human judgment. This is an additive causal clarification; it does not turn the circles into exclusive boxes or treat authority as earned merely by capability.
