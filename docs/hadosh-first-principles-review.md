# Hadosh Academy Through First Principles — publication review

## Baseline

The initial draft framed Hadosh through first-principles reasoning: human agency as the objective; replaceable model sessions; durable, user-owned harness state; explicit authority; protected boundaries; framework-specific implementations; and an education/production loop connecting Academy writing with Origin, Seed Agent, and Q-Seed.

## Issues found

- The “thousand-word harness” analogy could be detached from its surrounding thesis and misread as a search for one universal text.
- The relationship between natural-language exploration and deterministic runtime enforcement needed a sharper boundary.
- Modularity, local adaptation, pattern crystallization, and the Academy’s own evolving context were present but not connected into one causal argument.
- The future structured-contribution plugin needed to be clearly identified as a proposal rather than a current feature.
- The new mental-model series needed a public identity distinct from both Principles & Perspectives and the existing planetary Observation reader.

## Changes made

- Made the analogy explicitly local to one user, task, runtime, and accumulated body of work.
- Preserved the central thesis that Hadosh never prescribes one finished agent; it teaches transferable behavioral objectives and patterns from which each user builds a distinct harness.
- Connected the reasoning chain from foundational constraints to user ownership, modularity, local vocabulary, testing, and crystallization.
- Added bounded security language: implementation diversity may reduce exploit scaling but does not replace permissions, validation, isolation, review, or enforcement.
- Added the contribution-review plugin as a narrow future example with explicit user approval and destination-specific validation.
- Established “Hadosh Through Mental Models” as an analytical Observation series whose entries are current syntheses open to evidence-backed revision.
- Added four purpose-built storytelling visuals and a separate LinkedIn composition.

## Uncertainty and review boundary

This article is published as an exploratory synthesis, not a final architecture specification. Claims about future pattern crystallization and contribution-review plugins are proposals to test. Its narration remains intentionally unprepared until Hadi separately approves the prose. Comments and implementation reports are evidence and proposals, not automatic changes to the Academy’s accepted context.
