Ideation · family scale

Family Games

A private, family-owned persistent illustrated world for roughly 5–12 people: somewhere a family can return to, play together, create things, and leave memories that remain between gatherings.

Meet

Family members enter the same persistent world and can talk and spend time together rather than joining another generic video call.

Play

The world can contain animals, puzzles, games, creative activities, and things children can continue between family gatherings.

Create

Family members can name animals, record sounds, activate scenery, build structures, and gradually shape the world together.

Remember

The persistent world can become a place where shared creations and family memories accumulate over time.

Open question

What would make a family keep coming back?

The project is intentionally still at the concept stage. The useful questions are about rituals, live versus asynchronous play, what adults and children each contribute, and how memory can feel meaningful without becoming intrusive.
