Harnesses at different human scales

How does a digital cortex change when one user becomes a group?

The projects are intentionally different. Some are public Seed implementations for one person. Others are new harnesses built from scratch for a team, a family, or a larger collective. The point is not to force them into one product; it is to learn which abstractions survive changes in runtime, interface, authority, privacy, and number of people.

1Individual

CLI-centered personal Seed. The user owns the harness and grows jobs around their own work.

2–7Small team

Multiple local agents animate one shared private harness; a dashboard becomes the common team surface.

7–15Family

A private web world becomes the interface; communication, play, permissions, and family memory become central.

tens → ~100Collective

The harness cannot expect everyone to operate a dashboard. Email and lightweight participation become more useful interfaces.

These ranges describe the current design targets, not hard technical limits.

Shared harnesses

As more people join, the interface stops being the only thing that changes.

Shared memory, role boundaries, synchronization, privacy, governance, and who is allowed to change the substrate all become part of the architecture.

A collective factual-media project and the largest current harness-scale experiment. What these projects are testing

The stable abstraction should survive both a new runtime and a new social structure.

The implementation should change when the problem changes. The abstraction earns confidence only when it keeps making sense after those changes.

Ownership

Does the durable personalization layer remain with the user, team, family, or collective rather than becoming provider-only state?

Interface

When is a CLI appropriate, when does a dashboard help, when should the interface become a world, and when is email enough?

Authority

Who may change a personal behavior, a shared plugin, a family permission, or a collective governance rule?

Memory

What belongs to one person, what becomes shared, what should be public, and what must remain private for decades?

Agent multiplicity

Does one agent animate the harness, do many local agents share it, or does a central harness coordinate a broad human network?

Training and legibility

Can the people who depend on the system understand enough of its architecture to keep shaping it after the original builder leaves?
