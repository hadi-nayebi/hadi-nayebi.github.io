Why keep these experiments separate?

A personal harness, a six-person startup harness, a family world, and a hundred-person collective should not be forced into one implementation. The useful research question is which abstractions survive while ownership, interface, authority, and scale change.

Projects in early ideation stay intentionally sparse. Public discussion helps shape them before implementation creates accidental requirements.
