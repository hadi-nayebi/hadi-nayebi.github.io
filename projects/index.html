Project portfolio

One harness idea, three human scales.

These projects explore how user-owned cognition changes when the system belongs to one person, a small group, or a larger collective. Each project page begins with a plain-language overview, then opens into the architecture, source ideas, current stage, roadmap, and discussion.

Individuals

One person owns and cultivates the harness.

The primary surface is the CLI. A dashboard can be added for a particular deployment, while the individual Seed itself remains centered on the user's persistent cognition, jobs, and working memory.

02
Groups

The harness becomes a shared place.

For a handful of people, the web interface becomes central. Shared views, permissions, persistent state, synchronization, and household/team authority become part of the architecture.

Small-team implementationroughly 2–7 people

Team Harnesses

For startups and small project teams: one private repository, multiple local CLI agents, a shared dashboard, and a governed team-level harness built with the team so they can continue extending it themselves.

A private persistent family world. Family members meet as characters, talk spatially, discover and name animals, author parts of the environment, play live or asynchronously, and gradually build a shared archive that the family owns.

03
Collectives

Participation gets simpler as the crowd gets larger.

At tens or roughly a hundred participants, focused email requests, replies, and lightweight participation become the member-facing interface while the harness coordinates the deeper workflow.

Collective media experimenttens → roughly 100 contributors

Crime Cartography

A factual-media experiment where an agentic production harness works with a distributed human editorial layer. People contribute judgment, corrections, context, research leads, governance input, and eventually value-sharing decisions through focused participation.

Enter Crime Cartography
Email interfaceHarness → focused request → member reply → harness

The email layer lets contributors add judgment and expertise through familiar communication while the harness coordinates the underlying workflow.

What changes with scale

The interface changes first. The deeper architecture changes with it.

Individual

Local CLI, personal memory, personal authority, optional custom dashboard.

Group

Shared web surface, shared context, member roles, synchronization, governed substrate.

Collective

Low-friction participation, email requests, distributed human judgment, explicit governance and attribution.
