# Crime Cartography — agent brief

Version: public design v0.9
Updated: 2026-07-23

## What this is

Crime Cartography is a founder-led experiment in collective, AI-assisted media
production. Its production harness assembles sourced crime-data videos; Project
Subscribers can help review scripts, maps, visualizations, audio, and rough
cuts before publication.

Project subscription is an email membership on the Hadosh Academy website. It
is separate from subscribing to the YouTube channel. YouTube subscriptions,
views, likes, comments, shares, and watch activity do not earn points, money,
rank, or economic eligibility.

## What is operating

- A public crime-data and video-production repository.
- A 20-city inherited batch with 20 rendered videos.
- A private human review studio.
- A staged public-project design.
- A dedicated Crime Cartography visual identity.

## What is proposed

- Structured editorial tasks delivered by email.
- Peer review of human feedback.
- Contribution history and reputation.
- A war chest covering production and emergency costs.
- A legally reviewed contributor surplus pool and replaceable manager role.
- Milestone-based governance and a possible independent spin-off.

## What is unresolved

- Worker and contributor classification.
- Tax, payment, and gift-card operations.
- Whether beta contribution history has future economic value.
- The precise definition and accounting of distributable surplus.
- Governance, appeals, fraud controls, privacy, and intellectual-property terms.
- The milestone that should trigger the first public experiment update.

## Critique instructions

1. Separate factual claims, value judgments, proposals, and unresolved questions.
2. Identify incentive failures, ambiguous promises, safety problems, legal
   questions, and ways power could remain concentrated.
3. Suggest concrete language or mechanisms rather than only objecting.
4. Treat crime data and pre-publication material as sensitive.
5. Never infer that a public comment creates compensation, ownership, or a
   confidential relationship.
