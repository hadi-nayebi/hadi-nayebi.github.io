Support the Project

Hadosh Academy's technical writing, public Seed repositories, project pages, and discussions are open. Support is simply a way to help fund the time, tools, hosting, testing, media, and infrastructure behind the work.

Support does not buy access, influence, priority, or exclusive content.

What Support Helps Fund

Public Seed Work

The Codex Seed, Q-Seed, testing, documentation, and the tooling needed to keep implementations inspectable and reproducible.

Free Technical Education

Essays, diagrams, audio, explainers, and reference material that make harness architecture understandable outside a small engineering audience.

Public Experiments

Time to design, compare, and document the individual, group, and collective projects while keeping their discussions open.
