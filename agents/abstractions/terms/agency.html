Agency

Current definition

Agency is the capacity to form purposes, make judgments, and cause directed change. In Hadosh Academy, externalized agency means that part of a person’s practical capacity is expressed through a harness they can understand and govern. A useful harness helps the user preserve decisions, sustain responsibilities, apply learned methods, inspect consequences, and intervene when needed. Agent activity is not automatically user agency: more autonomous execution can reduce agency when visibility, correction, ownership, or the ability to stop are lost. The goal is therefore expanded human capacity, with the user retaining authorship and authority while the harness carries more of the operational burden.
