Model

Current definition

A model is a learned computational system that transforms supplied input into an inference, prediction, representation, decision, action proposal, or generated output. It provides learned capability, but it is not the complete agent. By itself, a model does not establish durable identity, local memory, job continuity, permissions, recovery, or ownership. Those responsibilities belong to the surrounding harness and runtime. A model may be general or specialized, remote or local, commercial or open-weight. Replacing it may change capability, cost, speed, privacy, and compatibility without necessarily replacing the user’s harness.
