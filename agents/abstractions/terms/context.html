Context

Current definition

Context is the selected information made available for interpreting and acting at a particular cognitive moment. It may include the current responsibility, relevant history, constraints, definitions, state, evidence, and authority. Context is not the same as memory, knowledge, a transcript, or every file the user owns; it is a temporary selection assembled from those sources. Durable information may exist without being loaded, and loaded information may not deserve preservation. Context quality depends on relevance, provenance, freshness, scope, and conflict handling—not only on token quantity. The harness stores and routes potential context, while the runtime presents a selected portion to the model.
