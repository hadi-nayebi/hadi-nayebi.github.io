Harness

Current definition

A harness is the durable operational structure around model capability that makes continuing, bounded, and inspectable work possible. It may hold selected context, instructions, memory, jobs, state, tools, permissions, interfaces, verification, history, and recovery mechanisms. A harness can be thin or extensive, local or partly distributed, mostly textual or heavily coded; it is defined by what it does rather than by one framework or directory layout. It is not merely a prompt, transcript, dashboard, CLI, or repository, although each may be part of one. The harness is the primary software surface through which a user’s methods, decisions, and working capacity can accumulate.
