Seed

Current definition

A seed is the smallest coherent context or substrate from which a distinct harness can grow through use. It gives a person and agent enough shared language, boundaries, and developmental direction to begin without pre-deciding the mature system. A seed is not a miniature finished agent. Additional structure should be earned by real responsibilities, evidence, and user decisions. A seed may be primarily contextual, as in this library, or may include a minimal executable substrate. Hadosh Academy provides the starting abstractions; each user and agent decide how those abstractions become files, behaviors, tools, permissions, and interfaces in their own harness.
