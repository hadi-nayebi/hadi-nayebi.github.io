Hadosh Through Mental Models

What becomes visible when the same evolving system is examined through different thinking tools?

This series applies one mental-model lens at a time to Hadosh Academy: its philosophy, architecture, projects, writing, and production process. Each lens adds a new view and checks earlier lenses against the context it develops. When later discussion or evidence improves an earlier explanation, that earlier article is revised with its history preserved. Questions without enough context remain open until later work can answer them responsibly.

Series method: preserve the accumulated thesis, change the angle of inspection, audit earlier lenses, and revise or leave questions open in proportion to the evidence.
