# AGENTS.md — Hadosh Through Mental Models

## Scope

These instructions apply to this directory and every descendant. The repository-root and `blog/AGENTS.md` instructions still apply.

## Purpose

This directory owns **Hadosh Through Mental Models**, an analytical Observation series that examines Hadosh Academy through one mental-model lens at a time. It is distinct from the visual-history format of *The Information System of a Planet* and from the numbered technical essays.

The series may grow across many lenses over time. Add one lens at a time; do not pre-write or lock a complete roadmap.

## Continuity rule

Every new lens must interpret the complete accumulated Hadosh thesis, not rebuild the thesis around the newest comment, analogy, project, or implementation. New evidence should change the synthesis only in proportion to its scope and strength.

In particular, preserve these established constraints unless Hadi explicitly changes them:

- Hadosh does not prescribe one universal harness or finished agent.
- Each harness is specific to its user, tasks, runtime, terminology, authority boundaries, and accumulated work.
- The Academy teaches concepts, behavioral objectives, patterns, and modular building blocks that users and their agents adapt locally.
- Public Seed repositories are sparse, framework-specific pattern-accumulation surfaces; Origin is a concrete implementation, not the universal target.
- Human agency is the objective. Agent autonomy is useful only within legible, user-governed boundaries.
- Writings are evolving public context. Comments and implementations are proposals and evidence; Hadi's review determines accepted revisions.

## Entry contract

Each entry must:

1. Define the lens in plain language and link to its source tradition.
2. State the question the lens asks of Hadosh.
3. Separate foundational constraints, current observations, proposals, and implementation examples.
4. Preserve uncertainty and identify what evidence could revise the conclusions.
5. Give readers a compact way to apply the lens to another topic.
6. Use only public evidence and protect the public/private boundary.
7. Include a canonical Markdown source, published HTML, review record, contribution surface, discovery metadata, and purposeful visuals.

## Publication state

An entry may be coherent enough to publish without being permanently final. Before narration or external promotion, Hadi reviews the website prose and visuals. Narration is derived only after prose approval and must follow the parent blog narration contract.
