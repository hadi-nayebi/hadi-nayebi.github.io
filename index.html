Your Digital Cortex

The model is the engine. The harness is the durable layer.

A Seed is the structure that stays with the user: memory, jobs, behavioral controls, cognitive modes, permissions, interfaces, and the experience accumulated through real work. Models can change underneath it while the user's architecture remains inspectable and portable.

The private Claude Seed is the first-generation reference. Seed Agent carries that architecture into Codex, Q-Seed explores a deeper open-framework version on Qwen Code, and the Projects portfolio tests how the same harness ideas change across individuals, groups, and collectives.
