Your Digital Cortex

The model is the engine. The harness is the durable layer.

If you change a review rule today, where will your agent find it next month? A harness can keep that rule alongside the jobs, decisions, permissions, and checks that guide its work. Put those parts where you can inspect and change them, and a new model can pick up more of what you have built.

The private Claude Seed provides one working source of behavioral evidence. The Academy writings abstract general patterns from it and other use. Seed Agent and Q-Seed are framework-specific accumulation surfaces for selected foundations; Origin is a separate public dashboard-and-harness substrate for one local user. The wider portfolio tests how these ideas change across individuals, groups, and collectives.
